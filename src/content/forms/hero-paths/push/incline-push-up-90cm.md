# Incline Push-Up — support ~90 cm

**Push horizontal · reps · bilateral**

*The first real lean. Hands on a worktop, feet on the balls: the body becomes a plank, and the brace starts to carry weight.*

## The movement

Hands on the edge of a stable surface at about 90 cm (kitchen worktop, solid table), body in one straight line from head to heels, feet on the balls. Lower the chest to the edge and press back to straight arms.

## Set-up — and what each part is for

- **Support** stable, heavy, and that cannot slide — a worktop against a wall is ideal. Check it before every set.
- **Hands** on the edge, slightly wider than the shoulders, fingers spread. Same hand position as the floor push-up.
- **Feet** on the balls, heels lifted, hip-width. Walk them back until the body is one straight line with the shoulders above the hands at lockout. **Measure your toe-to-support distance and reproduce it every session.**
- **Body** one straight line. Glutes squeezed, abdominals braced, ribs down. → *Feel a flat, hard panel from chest to pubis; no pinch in the low back.*
- **Shoulders** down, away from the ears; upper back wide.
- **Hands screwed** into the edge: an outward rotational intent without the hands moving. → *Feel like you push the support away, not just lock the elbows.*
- **Neck** long, eyes on the support just ahead of the hands.

## The rep — rehearse it before you do it

- **Down (1–2 s).** Elbows bend back at ~45°. → *Feel the load spread across chest and triceps, not piled onto the front of the shoulder.*
- **Bottom.** Chest touches or grazes the edge, brief still pause. → *Feel the trunk still rigid: the chest arrived first, the hips did not drop.*
- **Up.** Drive the support away to a complete lockout. Hips and shoulders rise together.

## Watch for

- **Hips sagging into a hammock** → brace before the descent, not during.
- **Head diving first** → long neck; the chest measures depth.
- **Elbows flaring to a T** → keep ~45°.
- **Partial range** → chest to the edge and full lockout, or the rep does not count.
- **Feet sliding** → change surface or re-measure the distance.

## Breathing

Inhale and brace at the top, hold the pressurised trunk through the descent, sharp exhale as you drive away from the bottom.

## Within the level

- *Harder:* feet together; 3 s descent; pause at the bottom.
- *Easier:* feet a little wider for a more stable base.

## Dose

Test a clean max; work at half of it (rounded down), max 5 reps per set, always leaving at least 2 reps in reserve. Stop each set well short of failure.

With the Muscle focus: your max minus 3 reps per set, never fewer than above, no cap of 5.
