# Wall Push-Up

**Push horizontal · reps · bilateral**

*The entry to the whole chain. Almost no load — the work is to learn the rigid line, the elbow path and the hand position you will keep all the way to the floor.*

## The movement

Standing, hands on a wall at shoulder height, body leaning in one straight line. Bend the elbows to bring the chest toward the wall, then press back to straight arms. Body, hips and head move as one block. No pushing ability is needed to start: if you can stand and lean, you can do this level.

## Set-up — and what each part is for

- **Face the wall**, feet about one arm's length away. The lean should feel light. **Measure this foot-to-wall distance and reproduce it every session** — a few centimetres change the load.
- **Hands** flat on the wall at shoulder height, slightly wider than the shoulders, fingers spread and pointing up. This sets the elbow path for every level that follows.
- **Feet** hip-width, heels down.
- **Body** one straight line, head to heels. Glutes squeezed, abdominals braced, ribs pulled down toward the pelvis. → *Feel a firm front panel from chest to pubis; the hips do not sag toward the wall.*
- **Shoulders** down, away from the ears.
- **Neck** long, eyes on the wall just above the hands.

## The rep — rehearse it before you do it

- **Down (1–2 s).** Elbows bend back at ~45° from the trunk, not flared out to the sides. The chest, not the face, travels toward the wall. → *Feel the load in the chest and the back of the arms, the whole line moving together.*
- **Bottom.** Chest a few centimetres from the wall, brief still pause. → *Feel the trunk still rigid: the hips have not dropped in first.*
- **Up.** Press the wall away to a complete lockout. Hips and shoulders return together.

## Watch for

- **Hips sagging toward the wall** → brace before the descent, not during.
- **Head or chin reaching for the wall** → long neck; the chest measures depth.
- **Elbows flaring to a T** → keep ~45°; it spares the shoulder.
- **Partial range** → chest close to the wall and full lockout, or the rep does not count.

## Breathing

Inhale and brace at the top, hold the brace on the way down, exhale as you press away.

## Within the level

- *Harder:* step the feet a little farther from the wall (heavier lean); 3 s descent; pause at the bottom.
- *Easier:* step closer to the wall.

## Dose

Test a clean max; work at half of it (rounded down), max 5 reps per set, always leaving at least 2 reps in reserve. Stop each set well short of failure. If your max is 2 or fewer, step closer to the wall and test again, or practice slow lowering only (3–5 s down, then step back to the start) until your max grows.

With the Muscle focus: your max minus 3 reps per set, never fewer than above, no cap of 5.
