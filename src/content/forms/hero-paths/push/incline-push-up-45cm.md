# Incline Push-Up — support ~45 cm

**Push horizontal · reps · bilateral**

*The last step before the floor. Most of the floor push-up's demand is already here — the brace and the full range are what you bring to the next level.*

## The movement

Hands on the edge of a stable surface at about 45 cm (sturdy bench, step, low solid box), body in one straight line, feet on the balls. Lower the chest to the edge and press back to straight arms.

## Set-up — and what each part is for

- **Support** stable and that cannot slide or tip. A bench against a wall, or a stair step, works well.
- **Hands** on the edge, slightly wider than the shoulders, fingers spread.
- **Feet** on the balls, heels lifted and driven back, hip-width. **Measure your toe-to-support distance and reproduce it every session.**
- **Body** one straight line, head to heels. Glutes squeezed, abdominals braced as if expecting a light punch, ribs down. → *Feel the front panel working hard now; the low back stays neutral.*
- **Shoulders** down; serratus (the muscle along the side of the ribs, under the shoulder blade) holding the blades flat to the ribs.
- **Hands screwed** into the edge.
- **Neck** long, eyes on the support just ahead of the hands.

## The rep — rehearse it before you do it

- **Down (1–2 s).** Elbows back at ~45°, forearms moving toward vertical. → *Feel the chest and triceps take most of the load, the lat catching the descent at the side of the trunk.*
- **Bottom.** Chest grazes the edge, brief still pause. → *Feel the trunk still rigid at the hardest point.*
- **Up.** Drive the support away to a complete lockout, shoulder blades spreading slightly around the ribcage.

## Watch for

- **Hips sagging** as the trunk tires → re-brace before each descent; end the set if the line breaks.
- **Head diving first** → the chest measures depth.
- **Elbows flaring** → keep ~45°.
- **Partial range at the bottom** → the chest reaches the edge.
- **Support moving** → stop and secure it.

## Breathing

Inhale and brace at the top, hold through the descent, sharp exhale as you drive out of the bottom.

## Within the level

- *Harder:* feet together; 3 s descent; pause at the bottom.
- *Easier:* feet a little wider; if the line keeps breaking, go back to the 90 cm level for a few days.

## Dose

Test a clean max; work at half of it (rounded down), max 5 reps per set, always leaving at least 2 reps in reserve, spread through the day. Stop each set well short of failure.
