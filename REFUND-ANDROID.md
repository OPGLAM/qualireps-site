# Refund Policy for Google Play

Last updated: 16 November 2026

## Language of this policy

This policy is published in English and in French. Both versions have the same value. If they differ, or if a passage is unclear, the interpretation most favourable to you, the customer, prevails.

## Which purchases

This policy covers the purchase of the full version in the Android app, made through Google Play. Payment and refunds of these purchases go through Google, under the Google Play terms.

## Within 48 hours

Within 48 hours of the purchase, you can ask Google Play for a refund directly, from your order history in your Google account or on Google Play's help page about refunds. Google decides on these requests.

## After 48 hours

After 48 hours, write to support@qualireps.app with your Google Play order number (it starts with GPA.). A refund after 48 hours is at our discretion. When we grant one, it is made through Google Play, to the payment method you used.

## Right of withdrawal in the European Union

The full version is digital content, unlocked as soon as the purchase is complete. Under European Union consumer law, the 14-day right of withdrawal does not apply to digital content once its supply has begun with your prior express consent and your acknowledgement that you thereby lose that right (Article 16(m) of Directive 2011/83/EU; in France, Article L221-28 of the Consumer Code). Where the law still gives you a right of withdrawal, we honour it: write to support@qualireps.app.

This does not affect your legal guarantee of conformity, described in our [Terms of Service](/terms): if the app does not work as described, write to us at support@qualireps.app.

## After a refund

After a refund, Google Play no longer reports the purchase. The app returns to the free version at its next start with a connection. Your practice data stays on your device; nothing is deleted.
