## In short

QualiReps has no account and collects no data about you. Your practice data stays on your device. We do not share data with advertisers — there are no advertisers.

## What we collect

### The app, on your device

The app asks for no account, no email, no name and no device identifier. It contains no analytics, no trackers and no third-party fonts.

Your practice data (exercises, sets, maxes, settings) is stored on your device: in your browser's storage (IndexedDB) for the web app, in the app's own storage for the Android app. In normal use, nothing leaves your device.

If you enter a license key, it is checked on your device, without any network connection.

### Practice reminders (Android app)

The Android app can remind you to practice. Reminders are off by default. They are computed and scheduled on your phone, from the data already on it; nothing is sent to us or to anyone else to produce them. When you turn them on, Android asks your permission to show notifications.

### Android's automatic backup (Android app)

Android can back up the data of your apps to your Google account. For QualiReps, this backup includes your practice data, so that a new phone signed in to the same Google account can restore it. The backup is made by Android and stored by Google, under Google's privacy policy; we have no access to it. The state of a purchase on Google Play is left out of it.

You can turn this backup off in your phone's settings (the Backup section of the Google or System settings, depending on the phone).

### When you buy on this website

Buying on this website is the one time data leaves the web app.

- The app loads Paddle's checkout, where you enter your email and payment details. Paddle.com Market Limited is the merchant of record: it holds your payment and contact details, under its own privacy policy.
- When the payment is complete, the app sends our license endpoint only the Paddle transaction id, and receives your license key in return. The key contains that transaction id and the purchase date, and no name or email. Paddle can link the transaction id to you.
- Paddle also notifies our server of the completed purchase. Our server then fetches your email address from Paddle and passes it, with your license key, to Resend, the email service that sends you the license key email.

We do not store your name, email, payment method, or any identifier on our servers.
<!-- /web-only -->

### When you buy on Google Play (Android app)

Google handles the payment, your Google account and the records of your purchase, under Google's privacy policy.

The app receives from Google Play a proof of purchase (the purchase details, signed by Google), checks the signature on your phone and keeps the proof there. It sends it to no one: no server of ours takes part in a purchase on Google Play. Google provides us, in its developer console, with the order details needed to manage the sale and refunds.

## Backups and exports

You can export your data as a backup file (JSON) or as CSV at any time. These files are saved where you choose (in the Android app, through Android's share sheet). We do not receive copies.

If you import a backup file, it is read locally by the application. No data is transmitted.

It also uses, without asking, what Android grants to every app that declares it: vibration (timed holds), keeping the screen on during a timed hold, starting after your phone restarts (to schedule reminders again), network access (for Google Play purchases only) and Google Play billing. You can withdraw the notification permission at any time in your phone's settings.

## Your rights

Your practice data is on your device, not with us. You can delete it at any time with Reset all data in the app, by uninstalling the app, or by clearing its data (in your browser for the web app, in Android's settings for the Android app). Android's backup in your Google account can be deleted from your Google account.
